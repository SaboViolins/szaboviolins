The maker

Four decades at the workbench

I began as a violin and viola player, then trained as a maker in Paris under the master Lengyel László. For 20 years I made and cared for instruments for musicians in Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia, before returning to Lorraine, near Mirecourt, where I work today.

Every instrument is made entirely by my own hands, from wood selection and preparation to the varnish, which I research and make myself.

I travel to Portugal regularly. That means I don't just sell an instrument and leave - I come back to adjust it, look after it, and see how it develops with its player.

The payment plan

How it works

These are fully professional instruments, not student models. The programme makes payment easier - it does not change the quality.

Try the instrument

The instruments are kept by teachers I work with in Portugal. Play one for two to three weeks, with your teacher, before deciding anything.

Sign a clear contract

One simple written agreement, in Portuguese, with every condition explained before you sign.

Make the start payment

The instrument is then yours to play, take to lessons, concerts and exams.

Then a fixed amount each month

By automatic bank transfer, with no interest and no fees. When the last payment is made, the instrument is fully yours.

Every instrument comes as a complete set: the instrument, a good quality bow, a case and rosin - plus a shoulder rest for violins and violas. Paying in full at once is of course also possible.

Questions

Good to know

How can you offer this?

Because I am the maker. Every instrument in the programme is made by me, in person, in my workshop - I don't buy instruments cheaply to resell them at a higher price. That gives me full control over the materials, every step of the making, and the way each instrument reaches its player. It also means I can decide myself how payment works, without a shop or a bank in between.

Who is the programme for?

Students and young professionals who are serious about music, usually recommended by their teacher. For students under 18, a parent or guardian signs the contract.

Who owns the instrument during the plan?

The instrument remains my property until the last payment, then becomes fully yours. In daily life nothing changes - you play it as your own.

What insurance is needed?

A musical instrument insurance covering theft, accidental damage and transport, for the full value. Several Portuguese insurers offer this; I can help you find one.

What if I need to stop?

We talk first. If you cannot continue, you return the instrument in good condition. The full conditions are written clearly in the contract.

Can I try several instruments?

Yes, depending on availability. Some instruments are already in Portugal with teachers I work with, and I bring others on my visits. Ask me which instruments are available at the moment.
